Our Story

More Than a Salon —
A Sanctuary

Founded in 2005, Barnatos Hairdressers has been a beloved destination for those who believe their hair is their finest accessory. Our team of passionate stylists combines technical mastery with creative vision to deliver looks that are as unique as you are.

From your first consultation to the final reveal, every visit to Barnatos is designed to be an experience — relaxing, inspiring, and utterly transformative.
